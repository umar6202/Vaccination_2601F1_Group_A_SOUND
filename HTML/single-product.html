Nightform was tracked over six weeks in a converted barn two hours north of the city, mostly live to tape with the doors open. What you hear on this pressing is the room as much as the band — the hum of the old Studer, rain on the roof of side C, the five of us standing close enough to breathe on each other.

We asked Bernie at Sterling to cut it loud but honest. The result is a record that rewards volume without flattening the quiet parts. If you only own one vebra artefact, we would like it to be this one.

Two 180g black vinyl records in a foil-blocked gatefold sleeve, printed on uncoated board with a matte varnish. Twelve-page insert, hand-numbered on the rear. Each copy carries a download card for 24-bit lossless files of the full album plus two unreleased takes.

  • Cut at 45rpm from original 2″ tape transfers
  • Pressed at Third Man, Detroit — edition of 1,000
  • Sleeve photography by Mara Okonkwo

Orders leave our Brooklyn studio within three working days in rigid record mailers. US shipping is free over $75; international rates are calculated at checkout and typically land within ten to fourteen days.
