02 June 2026 · Vienna, Austria

Wiener
Stadthalle

Doors 20:00 16+ Final tickets

One night, full volume

Vienna is the largest room on the Nightform run and the only European date where we play the record end to end. The Stadthalle has a six-second decay in the upper tiers, so we are bringing the full modular rig, a twelve-piece string section from the Musikverein, and the lighting design that closed Kings Theatre last winter.

Doors open at 20:00 with a short DJ set in the foyer. vebra take the stage at 21:00 and play for roughly one hundred minutes with no interval — the whole of side A in sequence, three songs from Halogen, and one thing we have never played live.

The venue is fully cashless and bag-checked. Anything larger than A4 goes in the cloakroom, which is free with a ticket. Under-16s must be accompanied by an adult, and the whole floor is standing except the accessible platform on stage left.
